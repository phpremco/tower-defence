Tower parity (TowerParityTests): value per coin of every buildable gun and level against four reference streams.
value = DPS x overkill x splash x (window + 2 x range / speed) / window, per 100 coins to reach the level
(the ledger's per-tower formula; hybrids cost both partners + recipe, combined at the cheapest level).
ratio = value / median of the towers that hit that layer at the same level; band 0.75 to 1.35.
upg = value / value of a new build (L0) of the same tower; floor 0.85. Not modelled: slow, beam lock, placement, permanent upgrades.
Streams (health incl. armour, speed, creatures per cell, window s):
  swarm  ground hp    40  speed 1.40  density 1.20  window 10
  heavy  ground hp   450  speed 0.70  density 0.15  window 12
  air    air    hp    45  speed 1.40  density 0.80  window 10
  boss   ground hp  2500  speed 0.50  density 0.00  window 4
Skipped: BaseTurret: base defence (bought with permanent upgrades, not coins); Blockade: gate (blocks ground creatures, set by the level); Cryo: slow mast (4.0 DPS): its worth is the time it buys the other towers; Support: support mast (no damage of its own)
