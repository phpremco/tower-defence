# Playtest notes, 2026-11-21 (round 88b)

What changed since the build you played on the 20th (round 87 with the 88a fixes), what to try, and what the
measurements already say is off. Details and all tables: `Docs/STATUS.md`, "Round 88b".

**Before you press Play: close and reopen the Unity editor once** if its console shows "d3d11: failed to create 2D
texture" (it did after the last two rounds; a restart clears it).

**Honest state:** everything you asked for is built and works by rule, but the balance is rougher than round 87's:
13 of 24 level-and-difficulty cells sit on their target (19 last time), because five new rules and harder Hard and
Psycho targets were tuned in one night. Treat this build as "try the new mechanics", not as "the numbers are final".

## What changed

- **The Cannon's blast hurts 2 creatures at level 1, 3 at level 2, 4 at level 3** (it was 3 on every level). The
  build bar and the tower menu say "Blast hits up to 2", and the upgrade line shows "2 > 3".
- **Your towers get shot at.** Every level boss fires at the three nearest guns within five cells every three
  seconds; Stingers, Shardbacks and the bats reach four to four and a half cells; the Cragback's slam reaches three.
  Skitters, Prowlers and Rollers still leave towers alone. Easy and Medium: scratches. Hard: expect to lose a gun or
  two if you never repair. Psycho: more (probably too much, see "Known").
- **A boss keeps sending its brood while it walks**, more the further it gets, as tough as the wave it came with, a
  quarter of it plated. A boss you kill at the first gate has sent almost nothing; one that gets past it fills the
  road behind it. The brood pays no kill money. The Core Wisp and the Pack Alpha now send a brood too.
- **Levels get tougher towards the end, more than before**: first waves softer, last waves tougher (a creature is
  2.8 times as tough at the end of a level as at its start; it was 1.8).
- **Less money from bosses**: a boss pays about a third of what it paid (Brood Mother 337 -> 126), so a mid-level
  boss on Hard is no longer a purse of four Cannons.
- **Hard and Psycho are aimed one step harder** than in round 87. Easy and Medium are aimed where they were.
- **Boss armour is a third of the bar** (it was half). The boss is smaller on the first levels and the same on the
  sixth; it brings the brood and the volley instead.
- **Fault Line Psycho**: its wave 13 (the swarm between two air raids) no longer carries a peak.
- **Surprise arrows** stay clear of the money line, the tower bar and each other.

## What to play, and what to watch

1. **Landing Site, Hard.** The question of the round: do you still end with a pile of money, and do the last waves
   (10 and 11) press harder than the middle? Put your guns between the roads as you did: does the boss of wave 8 hurt
   them, and do you have to repair or rebuild before wave 12?
2. **Glass Meadows, Medium, the boss.** Let the Brood Mother get past your first defence once on purpose. Is the road
   behind her dangerous now (the brood grows as she walks), and does she get to a gate? With Cannons at the first gate
   she should still die early, with little brood: is that still "sitting it out"?
3. **A Cannon row.** Level 1 Cannons against a dense swarm should feel clearly weaker than before; upgraded ones
   stronger. Is upgrading now the obvious move, and is a row of cheap Cannons still the answer to everything?
4. **Fault Line, Psycho.** It should be hard but no longer a wall at wave 13. The best bot now wins it at every
   strength we tried, so it may have become too soft for you: tell us.
5. **Crysium Vein, Easy.** Smaller boss, lighter armour, softer first waves. Still "like a Hard"?
6. **Any level, Psycho, for the tower damage.** The bots lose three to five of twelve guns a run there. Is that fun
   pressure (repair drone, rebuilding) or just annoying?

## Known before you start (measured, not fixed)

- **On Landing Site and Glass Meadows the last boss is still the only thing that beats the bots** (68 % and 90 % of
  their lost runs), and a Cannon-first build is still far stronger there than anything else. The blast cap did not
  change that: on those levels the gap is armour, not splash.
- **Uneven cells:** Landing Site Easy is very soft and Landing Site Medium tough for a player without Cannons;
  Glass Meadows Hard and Crysium Vein Medium are soft; Fault Line Medium is tough; Quartz Flats Psycho is too hard
  for every bot, Fault Line Psycho and Crysium Vein Psycho too soft for the best one.
- **Psycho tower damage is high.**
- A wrecked base turret cannot be repaired for the rest of the level (old gap, now easier to meet: the boss's volley
  reaches base turrets).
- The next-wave preview does not show a boss's brood. The new brood calls of the Pack Alpha have no sound of their own.
- Prowler scan text still says the Cannon's splash "hits the whole pack at once".

## What we would like to hear

- Money at the end of Hard and Psycho: how much was left, and did you have to choose in the last waves?
- Did a tower of yours get wrecked? Was repairing worth it?
- The boss wave: alive, or still a wait? Did a boss reach a gate?
- Is boss armour at a third of the bar enough to make you want Cannons, or do Gun Turrets now do it?
- Cannon 2 / 3 / 4: right, or should level 3 stay at 3?
