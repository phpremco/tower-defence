# Name brainstorm

Proposal, 2026-11-09. Remco decides. The working title is "Tower defense" (`productName`).

## How the names were checked

- Each name was searched once or twice (game title, "Steam", "game") on 2026-11-09.
- A clean search is **not** a trademark clearance. Before choosing, check:
  - the EUIPO and USPTO trademark registers (class 9 and 41);
  - a Steam store search;
  - the .com and Steam-handle availability.
- Rules:
  - no names or terms from Sentinel 4: Dark Star;
  - no generic names like "Crystal Defense" (crowded, and Square Enix has *Crystal Defenders*);
  - nothing that leans on a famous brand.

Conflict legend:
- **Clear**: no game found under this name.
- **Near**: a similar name exists; it's usable but worth a closer look.
- **Taken**: a game already uses this name.
- **Avoid**: a famous trademark or a real risk.

## 1. The living planet (the Burrowers, the planet wakes up)

| Name | Why | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Deepwake** | The planet wakes from the deep when we drill. That is the opening cutscene in one word. | Near: *DepthWake*, an ocean factory game (IndieDB, coming soon). No exact match. |
| **Undersong** | The crystals hum with the drill; something sings below. Poetic and quiet. | Clear as a game. Undersong is also a realm in a tabletop worldbuilding site (LegendKeeper). |
| **Burrowfall** | The Burrowers pour out of the ground; the colony falls or holds. | Clear. *Winter Burrow*, *Burrows* and *Burrow Wing* exist, but the names are different. |
| **Wakeworld** | A world that wakes up against us. | Clear. Some weak echoes, such as *Wakfu* and *Wake*. |
| **Living Ground** | Says the twist plainly. | Not searched in depth. It is generic and hard to own. |
| **Hollowheart** | The hollow planet with something sealed at its heart (The Core). | Not searched in depth. It is close to *Hollow Knight* in sound, so I would **avoid** it. |

## 2. The crystal (Crysium, the veins, the seal)

| Name | Why | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Veinbreak** | It means two things: we break open the crystal veins, and the seal breaks (twist B). It is short and active. | Clear. Near: *Veilbreak* (a jam game) and *Veil Break* (a small 2023 shooter). |
| **Shardline** | A crystal shard plus the defence line. It reads like a frontline. | Clear as a game. One itch.io developer uses "Shardline" as a studio name. Also near: *Shardbound* (card game). |
| **Crysium** | The game's own word for the crystals. It is unique to us. | Clear as a game. **Risk:** it looks and sounds close to *Crysis* (Crytek). I would not use it as the title, only in-game. |
| **Veinlight** | The glowing crystal veins on the planet map. | **Taken**: *VeinLight*, a survival horror game on itch.io and Steam. |
| **Crystal Veins** | Plain and descriptive. | Generic. The "Crystal" space is crowded (*Crystal Defenders*, *Crystal Guard TD*, *Crystal Rush*). |
| **Sealbreaker** | Names twist B. | **Taken**: *Sealbreakers* (Fire Sparrow Studio, 2026, Steam playtest). It would also spoil the twist. |
| **Shardfall** | Crystals and the capsules falling like stars. | Not searched in depth. "Shard" names are common. |

That is 25 candidates.

## Shortlist

1. **Veinbreak.**
   - It carries the whole story: we drill into the crystal veins, and the seal they form breaks.
   - It is two hard syllables, easy to say in Dutch and English, and has no direct conflict.
   - It also works as a verb for the crystal emblem: a vein cracking open.
2. **Shardline.**
   - The crystal and the defence line in one word, which is exactly what you build.
   - It reads clearly at capsule size.
   - The only clash is a small itch.io studio name.
3. **Deepwake.**
   - The planet waking from the deep is the hook of the opening cutscene.
   - It is a calm, ominous name that fits the dark, glowing look.
   - Check *DepthWake* before choosing.
4. **Undersong.**
   - The most distinctive and the most "living planet" of the set, and clean as a game title.
   - It is softer and does not say "action", so it may need a subtitle.
5. **Burrowfall.**
   - Clear, and it names the enemy and the invasion.
   - It reads more creature-feature than sci-fi.

**Logo concepts were made for the top 3** (Veinbreak, Shardline, Deepwake). See `ArtSource/Previews/Logo/` and `Tools/Blender/logo_concepts.py`.

A subtitle can carry the setting if needed, for example "Veinbreak: Defend S-69-CRYS". That keeps the code name in the story, not in the title.
