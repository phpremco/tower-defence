# Playtest notes, 2026-11-20 (round 87)

For Remco's test of this evening. What changed since the build you played on the 19th (round 86), what to look at, and
what the measurements already say will feel off. Details and all tables: `Docs/STATUS.md`, "Round 87".

**Before you press Play: close and reopen the Unity editor once.** After the import of this build its console shows
"d3d11: failed to create 2D texture" (the graphics device was lost while the headless test editors ran, as after
round 86); a restart clears it. The code compiles without errors.

## What changed

- **Waves follow one formula.** A wave's toughness is its place in the level (a steady rise from first to last wave),
  its kind (rush, heavy, raid) and two or three marked **peak waves** per level that are about a third tougher than
  their neighbours. The wave after a boss is a breather; the crowd that walks with a boss is light (about half).
  There is one tuning number per level instead of one per level and difficulty.
- **Level bosses have a size of their own**: bigger on every higher difficulty and in every later level, bigger the
  later in the level they come. Three times the toughest ordinary creature is only a lower limit now (it applies to
  four of the 42 level bosses).
- **Every boss carries as much armour as body.** Half of the bar you see is armour. Bullets and beams barely scratch
  armour (a fifth and a tenth), shells go through it. **A boss asks for the Cannon** (or the Railgun).
- **The Laser is the swarm gun.** Small pulses, very fast (9 a second at level 1, 15 at levels 2 and 3), on the next
  creature at once. The Gun Turret needs a moment (0.15 s) to swing to each new target, so a dense, fast swarm outruns
  it. The Laser is weak on armour and on bosses, and half again as strong on Wisps and Core Wisps (energy bodies).
  Price unchanged: 130 / 185 / 285.
- **Continent bosses** (the six arena finales) were sized again by the finale bot with their new armour; the bot beats
  all six on all four difficulties.

## What to play, and what to watch

1. **Landing Site, Easy.** The measurement says this is the *softest* cell of the build (the bots hold it up to twice
   the creature health). Watch: is it too easy now, or is this the Easy you asked for on the 18th? Do waves 4 and 8
   (the peaks) stand out? Is the Brood Mother of wave 12 (bar 1435, half armour) a fight or a formality?
2. **Landing Site, Hard.** On target for the careful bot (it breaks even just under the data). Two bosses: wave 8
   (bar 1757) and wave 12 (bar 2358); the peaks are waves 4 and 10. Watch: do you lose base health *before* the boss,
   or is the boss still the only thing that can beat you? In the bots' runs two thirds of all lost Landing Site runs
   still end in a boss wave (see "Known").
3. **Glass Meadows, Medium.** On target. Brood Mother in wave 12 (bar 2295). Peaks: waves 4 and 10. Same question:
   wave 10 should hurt.
4. **A laser-heavy try** (any level; Shard Ridge or Quartz Flats show it best, they send the swarms). Expectation:
   Lasers melt Skitter and Prowler streams, clearly better per coin than Gun Turrets there, and stall on Cragbacks
   and on the boss. On Landing Site Hard a Laser-first build loses (armour grows there wave after wave); that is the
   intended lesson, but tell us if it feels like a trap rather than a lesson.
5. **Cannon against the boss.** Take the same boss twice: once with Gun Turrets and Lasers only, once with two Cannons
   in reach of the last stretch. Expectation: without shells the armour half of the bar takes for ever; with Cannons
   the boss is routine. A Cannon-first build is by far the strongest way to play this build (see "Known").

## Known before you start (measured, not fixed)

- **Cannon-first is too strong.** The Cannon archetype holds Landing Site and Glass Meadows far above every other
  way of playing (on Hard: Cannon above x1.32, Laser-first below x0.75). With armoured bosses and armour that grows
  on these two levels, shells answer everything. The gate "Laser-first and Cannon-first within one step" fails in 10
  of 24 cells.
- **The boss wall is lower but still there on Landing Site and Glass Meadows** (65 % and 75 % of the bots' lost runs
  end in a boss wave; the aim was 35 %, the gate 40 %). The bots lose nothing in the peaks there. Shard Ridge, Fault
  Line and Quartz Flats are where the peaks do the work (21 to 37 % boss losses).
- **Fault Line on Psycho cannot be won by any bot.** Its only late ground wave (wave 13) sits between three air
  raids and takes the peak as a rush. A script question for that level; do not judge Psycho by it.
- **Glass Meadows on Psycho is too soft** for the skilled bot (it holds the whole ladder).
- **Easy is uneven:** Landing Site very soft, Glass Meadows and Crysium Vein a notch too tough for the weaker bot.
